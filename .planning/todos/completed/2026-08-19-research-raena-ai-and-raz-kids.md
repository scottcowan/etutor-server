## Problem

Two products that might have content, pedagogy, or UX patterns worth learning from or reusing:

- **Raena.ai** — AI-powered reading/literacy product (unclear on exact model)
- **Raz-Kids.com** (Learning A-Z) — large levelled reading library with guided reading, quizzes, and recordings; widely used in schools; has structured levelled book content (Level aa → Z2)

Neither can be copied directly (copyright), but they may:
1. Have open or licensable content (Raz-Kids has a subscription API)
2. Use pedagogical patterns worth replicating (levelled reading, running records, miscue analysis)
3. Have UX patterns for e-ink / read-aloud devices worth studying

## Solution

Research both:
1. Check if Raz-Kids / Learning A-Z has an API or licensing path for levelled book content — this would be very relevant for the Phase 4.1 corpus (book passages tagged to reading levels)
2. Look at Raena.ai's approach to AI-assisted reading — what does their tutor model look like compared to ours?
3. Identify any pedagogical patterns (running records, miscue analysis, phonics progression) not yet in our curriculum that are worth adding

Relevant phases: Phase 4.1 (corpus) and Phase 5 (child interface + device sync).
