# Phase 4.1 Plan 04: 21 Manipulation Topic Pages Summary

Authored all 21 L3 wiki topic pages for the Manipulation subject — the pilot content proof-of-concept resolving CORPUS-01's scope for this phase — each with D-03's H2 level-band structure (100/200/300/400/Sources/Related Topics/People), frontmatter matching `services/curriculum.py` exactly, and cross-links to the happiness-in-slavery source material and sibling prerequisite pages. Updated `docs/corpus/wiki/index.md` to list all 21 alongside the 9 previously-migrated pages.

## What Was Built

**Task 1 — Industry/doubt cluster (7 pages).** `tobacco_industry_lies`, `manufactured_doubt`, `merchants_of_doubt`, `food_industry_manipulation`, `pharmaceutical_marketing`, `propaganda_techniques`, `psyops_history`. Each has frontmatter (`topic_ids`, `subject: Manipulation`, `bloom_entry`/`bloom_target` copied from the matching `services/curriculum.py` `Topic` entry) and all four D-03 depth bands populated with genuinely topic-specific content — named techniques (bandwagon, glittering generality, transfer), documented history (the 1969 Brown & Williamson "Doubt is our product" memo, MKULTRA, the Master Settlement Agreement), and named academic frameworks (Oreskes & Conway's *Merchants of Doubt*, Ellul's propaganda theory). `propaganda_techniques` and `psyops_history` link the happiness-in-slavery source per the plan's explicit instruction.

**Task 2 — Attention/media cluster (7 pages).** `cia_covert_history`, `censorship_history`, `political_spin`, `attention_economy`, `media_ownership`, `astroturfing_fake_grassroots`, `dark_patterns_ux`. Content covers declassified CIA operations (Iran 1953, Chile 1973), UK/global censorship history, spin-doctoring, surveillance capitalism (Zuboff), media ownership concentration (Herman & Chomsky's propaganda model), astroturfing mechanics, and the dark-patterns taxonomy (Brignull, Gray et al.). `cia_covert_history` and `censorship_history` link the happiness-in-slavery source per the plan's explicit instruction (its Act 2/Act 3 state-control and information-suppression content).

**Task 3 — Psychology/defence cluster (7 pages) + index regeneration.** `nudge_theory`, `social_proof_manipulation`, `cult_techniques`, `radicalization_pipeline`, `inoculation_theory`, `recognising_manipulation`, `information_hygiene`. Covers Thaler/Sunstein nudge theory, Cialdini's persuasion principles and Milgram/Asch experiments, Lifton's thought-reform framework and the BITE model, online radicalisation pipeline research, McGuire's inoculation theory and modern prebunking tools, and the capstone `recognising_manipulation`/`information_hygiene` pair on lateral reading and information-diet habits. `cult_techniques` and `radicalization_pipeline` link the happiness-in-slavery source per the plan's explicit instruction (social-dominance-orientation and obedience-to-authority content); `social_proof_manipulation` also links it (see Deviations). `docs/corpus/wiki/index.md` was extended with one row per all 21 pages.

Every `## Related Topics` section lists each topic's `services/curriculum.py` `prerequisites` as markdown links — sibling Manipulation pages link directly (e.g. `manufactured_doubt.md` → `tobacco_industry_lies.md`), and cross-subject prerequisites not yet authored in this corpus (e.g. `scientific_method`, `cold_war_context`) are linked with an inline note flagging them as not-yet-authored, so the links are ready to resolve once those subjects get their own pilot passes.
